Enter the trip(s) you're dropping, then your candidate pickup trips. Each combination — one trip per day, never two on the same day — becomes one Adds row. With trips on N different days it defaults to one pickup per day (all options), chunked into groups of 10 for the iCrew Adds grid.

iCrew's Adds/Drops grid (the old Jacada page) only takes one cell at a time. The SWP Chrome extension gives you a mirror of that grid you can paste a whole block into (Excel-style spill, auto-formats dates to DDMMM, skips blank cells so it won't wipe existing Drops), then Fill Page writes it back into iCrew. Copy your rows from this Swap Builder, paste into SWP, fill.

  1. Unzip swp-v1_5.zip to a permanent folder (not Downloads/temp — Chrome reads it from where it sits).
  2. Open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode (top-right).
  3. Click Load unpacked and select the unzipped folder.
  4. Pin the pot icon to your toolbar.
  5. On iCrew's "Entry Of Swap With Open Time Request" page, click the icon, paste your block into the top-left Adds cell, hit Fill Page.

Desktop Chrome only (extensions don't run on the iPhone). Scroll iCrew to the right bank of rows (1–10, 11–20…) before filling.
